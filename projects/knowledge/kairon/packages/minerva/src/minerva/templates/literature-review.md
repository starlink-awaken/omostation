# Literature Review

> Systematic review of academic literature on a research topic.

**Default Level**: L2 | **Recommended Sources**: Scholar, arXiv (DDG + Exa for context)

## Sub-Questions

1. What are the foundational papers and seminal works on {{topic}}?
2. What are the major research directions and schools of thought in {{topic}}?
3. What are the key findings, methodologies, and datasets used in {{topic}} research?
4. What are the open problems and unresolved questions in {{topic}}?
5. What are the most cited recent papers (last 2 years) on {{topic}}?
6. How has the field evolved over time — what are the key turning points?
7. What interdisciplinary connections exist between {{topic}} and adjacent fields?
8. Which institutions and researchers are the most prolific in {{topic}}?

## Output Format

- **Field Overview**: Scope, history, current state
- **Seminal Works**: Top 10 papers with citations and impact summary
- **Research Taxonomy**: Major approaches categorized
- **Key Findings**: What we know with high confidence
- **Open Problems**: Gaps and active research questions
- **Methodology Survey**: Common methods, datasets, benchmarks
- **Future Directions**: Predicted trends and emerging areas
