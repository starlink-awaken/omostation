# Competitor Analysis

> Deep-dive competitive analysis of a product, company, or technology.

**Default Level**: L2 | **Recommended Sources**: DDG, Scholar, Exa, Metaso

## Sub-Questions

1. What is {{target}}'s core technology stack and architecture?
2. Who are {{target}}'s main competitors and how do they compare on features, pricing, and market share?
3. What are {{target}}'s key differentiators and competitive advantages?
4. What patents, research papers, or open-source contributions does {{target}} have?
5. What are the major risks, weaknesses, or criticisms of {{target}}?
6. What is {{target}}'s funding history, valuation, and business model?
7. What recent news, product launches, or strategy shifts have occurred around {{target}}?
8. What do independent analysts and industry experts say about {{target}}'s future?

## Output Format

- **Executive Summary**: 200-300 words
- **Technology Deep-Dive**: Architecture, stack, key innovations
- **Competitive Landscape**: Comparison table with 5+ competitors
- **SWOT Analysis**: Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats
- **Market Position**: Share, growth trajectory, TAM
- **Risk Assessment**: Regulatory, technical, market risks
