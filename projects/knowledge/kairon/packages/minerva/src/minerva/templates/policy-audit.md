# Policy Audit

> Comprehensive analysis of a policy, regulation, or legislative framework.

**Default Level**: L3 | **Recommended Sources**: DDG, Scholar, Metaso (regulatory databases)

## Sub-Questions

1. What is the full text, scope, and jurisdiction of {{policy}}?
2. What problem was {{policy}} designed to solve, and what evidence supported its creation?
3. Who are the key stakeholders affected by {{policy}} — industry, consumers, government?
4. What has been the measurable impact of {{policy}} since implementation?
5. What are the compliance requirements and enforcement mechanisms?
6. What criticisms, legal challenges, or reform proposals exist for {{policy}}?
7. How does {{policy}} compare to similar policies in other jurisdictions?
8. What unintended consequences or secondary effects has {{policy}} created?

## Output Format

- **Policy Summary**: Key provisions, scope, timeline
- **Stakeholder Analysis**: Who benefits, who bears costs
- **Impact Assessment**: Quantitative and qualitative effects
- **Compliance Framework**: Requirements checklist
- **Comparative Analysis**: International benchmarks
- **Reform Proposals**: Active debates and suggested changes
- **Risk & Uncertainty**: Implementation risks, legal challenges
