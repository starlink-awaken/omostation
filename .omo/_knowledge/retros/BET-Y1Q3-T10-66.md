content and a reversible Workspace quarantine.

## Q2. What happened?

The stable L4 inventory selected 58 runtime files and no cache or invalid
archive artifacts. All 58 moved to Workspace quarantine with a complete hash
manifest; the public-runtime family is now terminally retired in the branch.

## Q3. What went well?

unmatched active consumer. The transaction preflighted every source and
automatically restores earlier moves when a later boundary fails.

was intentionally unchanged; Workspace quarantine gained the same 58 files and
one manifest. No permanent deletion occurred.
