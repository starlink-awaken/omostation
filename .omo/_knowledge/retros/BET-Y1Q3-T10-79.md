# BET-Y1Q3-T10-79 retrospective

## Q1. What was intended?

Remove dangling cross-domain symlink objects from the Guozhuan Documents
runtime surface without reconstructing the missing public runtime.

## Q2. What happened?

Exactly 12 dangling symlinks were moved to a protected Workspace quarantine.
Their literal targets, modes, and link fingerprints were retained in a
manifest. No target bytes were restored and no permanent deletion occurred.

## Q3. What was discovered?

The same source directory also contains 5 content artifacts and 4 contracts;
the L4 classification was necessary to avoid moving them. The postflight source
tree has no runtime objects while its content/contracts remain available.

## Q4. Surface accounting

Documents lost 12 dangling symlink directory entries and Workspace gained one
12-link quarantine package plus manifest. Root progress evidence gained one
completed sub-scope. No content, contract, regular runtime, cache, schedule,
client, or public-runtime target changed.

## Q5. What should the next agent know?

Keep this manifest protected and use `lstat/readlink` for future verification.
The `work-runtime` family remains pending. The next analogous target is the
separate Guizi dangling-symlink surface; do not broaden this receipt to it.
