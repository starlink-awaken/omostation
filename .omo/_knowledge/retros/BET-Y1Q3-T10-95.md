# BET-Y1Q3-T10-95 retrospective

## Q1. What was intended?

Restore the completed T10-93 ledger entry after concurrent merge trees kept
the family registry and reports but dropped the corresponding BET record.

## Q2. What happened?

Comparison of the immutable T10-93 delivery head with current main proved the
ledger block was absent. The exact block was restored without touching the
family registry or host quarantine state.

## Q3. What was discovered?

Root ledger loss can occur independently from document and registry survival
during concurrent squash/merge-group operations. Mainline verification must
check every declared write surface, not only the PR merge status or a subset of
the evidence files.

## Q4. Surface accounting

The recovery adds one missing T10-93 ledger block plus four T10-95 evidence
surfaces. It adds no runtime payload, host file, registry mutation, submodule
pointer, or product value evidence.

## Q5. What should the next agent know?

After T10-95 reaches mainline, verify that T10-92, T10-93, and T10-94 all
exist in the same ledger tree before closing their workflow runs. Continue
family owner implementation separately; this BET only restores accounting
truth.
