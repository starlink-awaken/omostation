# BET-Y1Q4-T10-165 retro — OMO persistent Role/Capsule/Handoff/Claim/Verification/ASD

## What changed

- Persistent `RoleRegistry` and admission state machine, including monotonic
  versions, stale-version rejection, canonical digesting, and fail-closed load.
- Immutable Capsule envelopes with WorkPacket and receipt digest binding.
- Capsule handoff now verifies before recording `HandoffRecorded` in Workflow
  Mesh; an invalid Capsule never reaches the evidence plane.
- task_gateway now supports claim-bound submission with WorkPacket digest and
  Claims Authority receipt digest persistence, SQLite migration, and CLI flags.
- Role verification now exposes a verifier interface with a stable source digest
  and composes additional verifiers only after admitted/capability checks.
- ASD five-panel contracts and role-admission gate reporting feed the
  observability surface while preserving the no-gate-greenwashing rule.
