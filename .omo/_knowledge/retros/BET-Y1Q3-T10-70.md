# BET-Y1Q3-T10-70 retrospective

## Q1. What was intended?

Physically remove the remaining executable cockpit runtime surface from
Documents while preserving content and rollback.

## Q2. What happened?

The L4 audit selected 9 runtime files and no cache/invalid artifacts. They were
moved to a protected Workspace quarantine. Source/target hashes, sizes, modes,
and a durable manifest were verified immediately and after a delay.

## Q3. What went well?

Fresh preflight and postflight consumer audits both showed zero forbidden
executors and zero unmatched consumers. The earlier lib root-anchor bug was
caught by RED/GREEN testing before this physical move.

## Q4. Surface accounting

Documents runtime surface reduced by 9 files / 25769 bytes. Content,
contracts, projections, bridge material, and `.bak` material were unchanged.
Workspace quarantine gained 9 files and one manifest; no permanent deletion
occurred.

## Q5. What changes next?

Keep cockpit quarantine under retention observation. Do not process the next
family until the public-runtime loss is either recovered from a trusted source
or explicitly accepted as irreversible data loss; continue only with fresh
receipts and protected targets.
