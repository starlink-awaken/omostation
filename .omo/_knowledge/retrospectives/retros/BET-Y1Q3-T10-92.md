# BET-Y1Q3-T10-92 retrospective

## Q1. What was intended?

Remove the two unconsumed OPC executable helpers from the Documents execution
plane while retaining a safe rollback package and honest owner-parity state.

## Q2. What happened?

The L4 classifier selected exactly `cmm.sh` and
`setup-codebase-memory-mcp.sh`. Consumer audit found no active executor for
them, and the reversible quarantine owner moved both files into Workspace
retention with a hash-valid manifest.

## Q3. What was discovered?

OPC content, contracts, projections, and facts remain in Documents; only the
two executable helpers were runtime artifacts. Physical removal is safe for
the observed consumers, but it is not equivalent to installing a Toolbox or
Workspace replacement.

## Q4. Surface accounting

Documents lost two runtime files totaling 6403 bytes; Workspace gained one
recoverable quarantine package and its manifest. No OPC content/contract/
projection/fact file, client state, or unrelated runtime surface changed.

## Q5. What should the next agent know?

Keep `opc-tools` at `in_progress` until an accepted owner command and
pointer-only guidance exist. If the setup capability is needed again, restore
only from the manifest after rechecking the recorded hashes and consumer state.
