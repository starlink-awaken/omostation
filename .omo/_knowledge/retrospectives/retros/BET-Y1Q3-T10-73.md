# BET-Y1Q3-T10-73 retrospective

## Q1. What was intended?

Remove dangling execution-plane symlinks left by the public-runtime loss,
without following or reconstructing target bytes.

## Q2. What happened?

The owner initially rejected symlinks safely. T10-73 added no-follow link-object
support and moved exactly 12 dangling Weijian links into protected quarantine;
17 regular runtime files remained untouched.

## Q3. What went well?

The missing target condition was explicitly tested. Fresh consumer evidence,
link identity, source absence, and delayed manifest retention all passed.

## Q4. Surface accounting

Documents symlink surface reduced by 12 link objects; target bytes changed by
zero. Workspace quarantine gained 12 symlinks and one manifest. No regular
runtime/content surface or permanent deletion was introduced.

## Q5. What changes next?

Keep the symlink quarantine retained. Process the 17 regular Weijian runtime
files only in a separate transaction after consumer parity and rollback checks;
the broader work-runtime family remains non-terminal.
